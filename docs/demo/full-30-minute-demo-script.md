# Full 30 Minute Demo Script

Use this for a serious prospect or pilot meeting. Keep the pace practical and avoid over-explaining technical details.

## 0-3 Min: Business Pain Introduction

What to say:

"Most freight forwarders are already doing the work, but the information is split across Excel, WhatsApp, email, paper files, and accounts sheets. Owners struggle to answer simple questions: which files are delayed, which documents are missing, which deliveries are waiting for POD, and which delivered jobs are still not financially closed."

Role benefit: Owner/Admin.

Expected reaction/questions:

- "Can it match our current file process?"
- "Can different teams see only their own work?"
- "Can clients track without calling us?"

## 3-7 Min: Dashboard And Operational Control

What to click:

- Login at `/login`
- Open Dashboard
- Show KPIs, operational health, sales/request flow, and Quick Actions

What to say:

"This dashboard is the manager's daily control center. It tells the team what needs attention: missing documents, delayed jobs, delivery/POD issues, finance closeout pending, and outstanding receivable or payable work."

Role benefit: Owner, operations manager, accounts lead.

Expected questions:

- "Can we filter by branch or team later?"
- "Can we add our own KPI names?"

## 7-12 Min: Shipment / Job File Lifecycle

What to click:

- Open Shipments
- Open `JOB-DEMO-2026-0001`
- Show current status, customer, route, service scope, documents, workflow, and finance context

What to say:

"The job file is the center of the operation. Every team can open the same file and understand what is happening without asking five different people."

Role benefit: Operations Officer, Documentation Officer, Accounts Officer.

Expected questions:

- "Can we add fields specific to our company?"
- "Can each department update only its own section?"

## 12-16 Min: Quotation And Costing

What to click:

- Open Quotations
- Show `QT-DEMO-2026-0001`, `QT-DEMO-2026-0002`, and `QT-DEMO-2026-0003`
- Explain pending, accepted/converted, and rejected stages

What to say:

"The sales team can track customer quotes and conversion. Accepted quotations can connect to job files, so sales and operations do not lose handoff details."

Role benefit: Sales Executive, Owner/Admin.

Expected questions:

- "Can quotation format match our current letterhead?"
- "Can we compare buy cost and sell price?"

## 16-20 Min: Document Center And Freight Documents

What to click:

- Open a shipment's document area
- Open a freight document detail page
- Show generated documents, uploaded/external documents, client-visible marker, locked/read-only state if present

What to say:

"Documentation is often where mistakes happen. This section shows what is generated in-system, what is uploaded from outside, what is visible to the client, and what is internal only."

Role benefit: Documentation Officer, Operations Officer.

Expected questions:

- "Can HBL/HAWB format match our company?"
- "Can client documents be controlled one by one?"

## 20-23 Min: Delivery / POD Workflow

What to click:

- Open workflow/delivery section
- Show Delivery Order, Gate Pass, cargo release, out for delivery, delivered, POD pending/verified

What to say:

"Delivery and POD tracking prevents a common problem: cargo is delivered, but proof, billing, and closeout are still scattered."

Role benefit: Operations Officer, Delivery Coordinator, Accounts Officer.

Expected questions:

- "Can we require POD before closing?"
- "Can port-to-port jobs skip POD?"

## 23-26 Min: Finance Closeout And Final Profit

What to click:

- Open financial report or a finance locked job such as `JOB-DEMO-2026-0005`
- Show invoice, vendor bill, payment, pending receivable/payable, closeout, and final profit snapshot

What to say:

"Finance closeout locks the final commercial result after operations are complete. Owners can see final profit, but this information stays protected from client portal users."

Role benefit: Owner/Admin, Accounts Officer.

Expected questions:

- "Can payment be posted after lock?"
- "Can non-finance users be blocked from profit?"

## 26-28 Min: Report Center And Analytics

What to click:

- Open Report Center
- Open Operations, Documents, Workflow/Delivery, and Financial reports

What to say:

"Reports are built around real management questions: workload, document gaps, delivery progress, quotation conversion, receivable/payable, and profit."

Role benefit: Owner/Admin, managers.

Expected questions:

- "Can we export to Excel?"
- "Can we add monthly management reports?"

## 28-30 Min: Client Portal

What to click:

- Open `/portal/demo-freight/login`
- Login with `DFC-CL-2026-0001` / `Admin123`
- Show client-safe shipments and documents

What to say:

"The client portal reduces follow-up calls. Clients can see shipment progress and safe documents, while internal cost, vendor payable, profit, margin, and notes stay hidden."

Role benefit: Customer service, clients, owner.

Expected questions:

- "Can each client have separate login?"
- "Can the portal use our logo?"

## Closing: Pilot Proposal

What to say:

"The best next step is a short pilot with your real process mapped into the system: a few users, a few live shipment files, document templates, reporting requirements, and feedback from operations, documentation, accounts, and one client."
