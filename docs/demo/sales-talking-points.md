# Sales Talking Points

## One Line Pitch

Freight Control is a freight forwarding operating system that connects quotations, shipments, documents, delivery, finance closeout, reports, and client tracking in one secure workflow.

## 30 Second Pitch

"Most forwarding teams work from Excel, WhatsApp, emails, and separate accounts files. Freight Control brings the full job file into one system: quotation, shipment, documents, delivery/POD, billing, finance closeout, reporting, and client portal. Owners get visibility, teams get a clear workflow, and clients get safe tracking without seeing internal cost or profit."

## Common Problems

- Job status is scattered across people and spreadsheets.
- Owners cannot quickly see which files need attention.
- Documents are missing, duplicated, or sent manually.
- Delivery/POD status is not clearly connected to finance closeout.
- Accounts teams struggle to know which delivered jobs are still open.
- Clients keep asking for shipment updates by phone or WhatsApp.
- Profit visibility is not protected enough in manual files.

## Solution Points

- One company dashboard for workload and exceptions.
- Shipment/job file as the operational source of truth.
- Quotation to job handoff for sales and operations.
- Freight document and uploaded document tracking.
- Delivery Order, Gate Pass, cargo release, delivered, and POD workflow.
- Invoice, vendor bill, payment, and finance closeout tracking.
- Role-based access and client-safe portal views.
- Reports for operations, documents, workflow, quotations, and finance.

## Key Benefits

- Faster owner visibility.
- Less manual follow-up between teams.
- Cleaner document control.
- Safer client communication.
- Better delivered-but-not-closed tracking.
- Protected finance and profit visibility.
- Stronger pilot foundation for company-specific workflow.

## ROI / Value Points

- Save time spent preparing status reports manually.
- Reduce missed document and POD follow-up.
- Reduce delayed billing after delivery.
- Improve collection follow-up with clearer receivable status.
- Give clients self-service visibility without exposing sensitive data.
- Help owners measure team performance and file profitability.

## Why Replace Excel / WhatsApp / Manual Process

Excel is flexible, but it does not enforce workflow, security, document visibility, or live role-based reporting. WhatsApp is useful for communication, but it is not a system of record. Freight Control keeps the operation traceable while still fitting the way forwarding teams think about job files.

## Bangladesh Freight Forwarding Market Fit

- Forwarders often manage many small and medium customers with high coordination pressure.
- Teams rely heavily on WhatsApp, email, Excel, and manual document folders.
- Owners need visibility across operations, documentation, delivery, accounts, and client service.
- Client portal can become a strong differentiator for local forwarders serving exporters/importers.
- The system can support freight-specific terms like HBL, HAWB, MBL, MAWB, Delivery Order, Gate Pass, POD, receivable, payable, and final profit.

## Client Objections And Replies

### "We already use Excel."

Reply: "Excel can continue for analysis, but Freight Control gives controlled workflow, document visibility, permissions, dashboard signals, and client portal access that Excel cannot safely manage."

### "Our process is different."

Reply: "That is exactly why a pilot is useful. We map your current file process first, then configure the screens, roles, document flow, and reports around your operating style."

### "Will my client see internal cost?"

Reply: "No. The portal is client-safe. Vendor cost, buy cost, profit, margin, internal notes, and vendor payable data stay protected."

### "Can we get Excel/PDF exports?"

Reply: "Export packs are on the roadmap. The current demo focuses on getting operational data clean and controlled first, then exports become much more valuable."

### "Will this replace our accounting software?"

Reply: "Not immediately. Freight Control handles freight file finance closeout, invoices, vendor bills, payments, receivable/payable visibility, and profit snapshots. Deep accounting integration can be planned separately."
