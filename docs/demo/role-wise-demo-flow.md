# Role Wise Demo Flow

## Owner / Admin

- Screen to show: Dashboard, Report Center, Financial Report, finance locked job
- Feature that matters: management visibility, workload, delays, finance closeout, final profit
- Business pain solved: no need to chase every department for file status and profit status
- What not to show: platform-only setup screens unless requested

## Operations Officer

- Screen to show: Shipments, shipment detail, workflow/delivery section
- Feature that matters: job status, route, delivery stages, missing requirements, POD
- Business pain solved: reduces manual coordination and missed operational follow-up
- What not to show: final profit, vendor margin discussion, platform settings

## Sales Executive

- Screen to show: Quotations, quotation detail, accepted/converted quote
- Feature that matters: pending quotes, accepted quotes, rejected quotes, conversion to job file
- Business pain solved: sales handoff to operations becomes traceable
- What not to show: internal final profit unless the user has finance authority

## Documentation Officer

- Screen to show: shipment documents, freight document detail, document reports
- Feature that matters: HBL/HAWB, Manifest, Customer Debit Note, Delivery Order, Gate Pass, POD, client-visible flag
- Business pain solved: fewer missing document surprises and clearer document ownership
- What not to show: buy cost, vendor payable, internal finance fields

## Accounts Officer

- Screen to show: invoices, vendor bills, payments, finance closeout, financial report
- Feature that matters: receivable, payable, pending payment, close-ready jobs, locked finance result
- Business pain solved: delivered files do not stay financially open unnoticed
- What not to show: client portal credentials or platform administration unless relevant

## Client Portal User

- Screen to show: client portal shipment list, shipment detail, safe documents
- Feature that matters: client can track shipment status and view permitted documents
- Business pain solved: fewer phone calls and WhatsApp follow-ups for basic status
- What not to show: vendor cost, buy cost, gross profit, final profit, margin, internal notes, vendor payable, raw file paths
