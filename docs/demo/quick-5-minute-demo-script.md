# Quick 5 Minute Demo Script

Use this when the owner is busy and wants to know quickly whether the system solves daily freight forwarding pain.

## 1. Login To Company Dashboard

Click: `http://localhost:3000/login`

Use: `admin@freightcontrol.com` / `Admin123`

Say: "This is the owner and operations control center. Instead of checking Excel, WhatsApp, accounts files, and document folders separately, the team can start from one dashboard."

## 2. Show Dashboard KPIs And Quick Actions

Click: Dashboard.

Say: "At the top, management can see workload, urgent issues, missing documents, delivery/POD status, and finance closeout signals. Quick Actions take the team directly to common daily tasks."

Role benefit: Owner, operations manager, accounts lead.

## 3. Show Report Center

Click: Report Center.

Say: "Reports are grouped by business questions: operations, quotations, documents, workflow/delivery, and finance. The goal is not just data entry; the goal is management visibility."

## 4. Show One Shipment / Job File

Click: Shipments, then open `JOB-DEMO-2026-0001` or another demo job.

Say: "Each job file keeps shipment details, current status, documents, delivery progress, billing, and closeout in one place."

## 5. Show Documents

Open the document area or a freight document from the job.

Say: "The documentation team can see what is generated, what is uploaded externally, what is client-visible, and what is internal."

## 6. Show Delivery / POD

Open workflow or delivery/POD section.

Say: "For delivery jobs, the team can track Delivery Order, Gate Pass, cargo release, delivered status, and POD verification."

## 7. Show Finance Closeout / Final Profit

Open financial report or finance closeout area as an authorized company user.

Say: "After delivery, finance can close the file with receivable, payable, and final profit visibility. Final profit is protected and not shown to portal users."

## 8. Show Client Portal Tracking

Open: `http://localhost:3000/portal/demo-freight/login`

Use: `DFC-CL-2026-0001` / `Admin123`

Say: "Clients can track their own shipments and safe documents without seeing internal cost, vendor payable, profit, margin, or internal notes."

## 9. End With Value Statement

Say: "Freight Control gives the company one operating system for shipments, documents, delivery, finance closeout, reporting, and client visibility. It reduces manual follow-up and gives management a live control view."
