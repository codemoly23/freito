# Follow Up Templates

These are practical starting points for demo follow-up. Keep the tone human and adjust to the relationship.

## WhatsApp Follow-Up After Demo

English:

Hi [Name], thank you for your time today. As discussed, Freight Control can help your team manage shipment files, documents, delivery/POD, finance closeout, reporting, and client tracking in one place. A good next step would be a short pilot with a few live files and your actual document/report requirements.

Banglish:

Hi [Name], ajker demo-r jonno thanks. Amra jeita discuss korlam, Freight Control apnader shipment file, documents, delivery/POD, finance closeout, reports, and client tracking ek jaygay manage korte help korbe. Next step hisebe choto ekta pilot korte pari, kichu live file and apnader real document/report requirement niye.
